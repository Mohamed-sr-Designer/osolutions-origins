Enhance & Process

Six designers.
Two teams.
Ninety days.

Every person walks their own road, one stop at a time — each stop opens the next. The shared core is identical for all six, the team track adds the craft your team needs, and your personal track is chosen for the unlock you are closest to. Every track is capped at 3 months.
